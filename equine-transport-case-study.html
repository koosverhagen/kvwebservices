Equine Transport UK — Astro Website & Custom Booking Platform

A complete digital platform for self-drive horsebox hire and driven horse transport, combining a modern public website with live booking, payments, customer communication and practical day-to-day admin tools.

The Business Need

Equine Transport UK needed more than a brochure website. Customers needed a straightforward way to choose a lorry, check availability, book and pay, while the business needed reliable control over bookings, deposits, forms, customer records and vehicle availability.

The public website also needed to become faster, easier to maintain and better structured without interrupting the live booking operation.

What Was Delivered

  • Mobile-first Astro website hosted by Cloudflare Pages
  • Custom booking assistant and live availability flow
  • Protected admin system for bookings and customers
  • Stripe payments, deposit holds, balances and refunds
  • Booking forms, confirmation emails and customer links
  • Vehicle calendars and practical mobile admin tools
  • Light and dark themes with accessible page structure
  • Staged migration and production release validation

One Connected Platform

Custom Booking & Admin Platform

The booking platform was built around the real workflow of a horsebox hire business rather than forcing the business into a generic booking template. Customers can choose the correct hire option, check live availability and complete the required booking steps online.

Behind the public journey, the protected admin system brings together bookings, customers, vehicle availability, forms, payments, deposits and common follow-up actions in one practical place.

Payments & Complete Booking Lifecycle

Stripe is integrated with the booking workflow for confirmation payments, outstanding balances, deposit holds, captures and refunds. Webhooks keep the booking record aligned with payment activity and support the emails and customer links needed after checkout.

Before the final website release, the live journey was checked from booking and payment confirmation through to customer email, refund and cancellation behaviour.

Astro Conversion on Cloudflare Pages

The public presentation layer was rebuilt in Astro and deployed on Cloudflare Pages. The result is a lightweight, maintainable website with clear content structure, responsive layouts and less reliance on client-side code for ordinary pages.

The existing booking services remained in place, so the migration improved the website without changing established booking IDs, payment behaviour or day-to-day admin processes.

Safe Staged Migration

The Astro version was built and tested in a separate staging setup before production cutover. Booking connections, forms, payments, emails, redirects and key customer journeys were checked in stages.

This reduced release risk and allowed the previous public site to keep serving customers until the replacement was ready.

Mobile-First Customer Experience

The website and booking journey were refined for phone use as well as desktop. Clear navigation, readable content, responsive booking steps and light/dark themes help customers use the service comfortably across devices.

Structured content, semantic page hierarchy, accessibility checks and performance-conscious implementation support both people and search systems.

Outcome

Equine Transport UK now has a modern Astro production website connected to a mature custom booking and admin platform. Customers have a clearer route from information to booking, while the business retains the operational tools and payment lifecycle already proven in daily use.

The project demonstrates how a public website can be modernised safely while protecting the business systems behind it.
